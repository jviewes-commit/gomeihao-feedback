# GoMeihao Feedback & Community Hub 🇺🇸

[简体中文](README.md) | [English](README-en.md)

Welcome to the official public issue tracker for **[GoMeihao (gomeihao.com)](https://gomeihao.com)**.

GoMeihao (Go 美好) is a free, offline-first kit for **Chinese visitors to the US and parents visiting their children here**: EVUS and customs checks, big English show-cards (doctor, pharmacy, restaurant, transport, hotel, police, emergency), tip and sales-tax calculators, a medical triage guide, US/Beijing dual clocks, 911 and Chinese consulates. California first.

---

## 💡 How to Give Feedback

- 🛂 **Correct an entry or policy rule**: [Submit Policy Correction](https://github.com/jviewes-commit/gomeihao-feedback/issues/new?template=01_policy_correction.md)  
  *EVUS, customs, I-94, sales tax, traffic rules, consulate districts. Please include an official source (CBP, USCIS, state government, Chinese embassy or consulate).*
- 🗣️ **Fix or suggest a show-card**: [Submit Card Feedback](https://github.com/jviewes-commit/gomeihao-feedback/issues/new?template=02_card_content.md)  
  *English that sounds unnatural or confusing to Americans, a wrong Chinese gloss, or a phrase that's missing. Native English speakers are especially welcome.*
- 🐛 **Report a Bug**: [Open Bug Report](https://github.com/jviewes-commit/gomeihao-feedback/issues/new?template=03_bug_report.md)  
  *Layout glitches, offline mode not working, no audio, wrong time, install problems.*
- 💡 **Suggest a Feature**: [Submit Feature Request](https://github.com/jviewes-commit/gomeihao-feedback/issues/new?template=04_feature_request.md)

No GitHub account? Email [support@gomeihao.com](mailto:support@gomeihao.com).

## 🔍 How We Check Facts

- Entry, customs and legal facts come only from official sources: CBP, USCIS, the Federal Register, state government sites and Chinese diplomatic missions in the US.
- CBP officers make the final decision on entry. GoMeihao is guidance, not legal or medical advice.
- Corrections are checked against the official text before they ship; every policy item in the app shows its source and review date.

---

## 🔒 Privacy

- **No account, no ads.** Everything you save stays on your phone.
- **Anonymous visit counts only**, via Cloudflare Web Analytics: no cookies, no cross-site tracking.
- **Works offline** once opened, including the show-cards and emergency numbers.
- Please don't post passport or visa numbers, phone numbers, addresses or booking references in issues.
- Full policy: [Privacy & Safety](https://gomeihao.com/#/privacy)
